My project notes

What I made and what I learned

A short explanation I can share when someone asks about this project.

What did I make, and why?

I made a one-page GDG campus stall site with a small, auto-scored tech quiz. It gives students a quick, friendly way to try something and learn how to join a team.

How will I collect sign-ups?

I’ll use a Google Form QR code with name, contact email, and preferred team. Its linked Sheet lets the organizers sort interested students by team.

What will catch students’ interest?

The one-minute quiz: students can play solo or challenge a friend, get an instant score, and talk about the answers with the team.

What was difficult, and what do I want to learn?

Keeping it simple while making it useful was difficult. In GDG, I want to learn how to build projects with a team and improve my web development skills.

Surprise plan: the projector stops working

I’d stay calm, tell the audience we can continue without slides, and switch to a short spoken introduction. I’d use the quiz on a phone or laptop if available, invite a show of hands, and share the QR code or a printed sign-up sheet afterward.
